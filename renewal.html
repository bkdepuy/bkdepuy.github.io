On the renewal message

The physics framework and the renewal writing are not separate projects. The Medium argues that reality is irreducibly active, that distinction and relation are the primitives of everything, that emergence is the fundamental story of the universe. The Levy County sanctuary, the crow relationships, the naturalist practice, the life lived on its own terms — these are the same argument lived rather than formalized.

The renewal message is simply this: pay attention. Build things that take time. Take opportunities when they come. The world is more active and more relational than any framework can fully capture — but the attempt to capture it honestly is worth making.
